About the PHQ-9 and scoring

The PHQ-9 measures depressive symptoms over the past two weeks. Add the nine answers to get a score from 0 to 27. Higher scores indicate more symptoms. A score of 10 or more is a reason to discuss the result with a qualified health professional. The separate question about daily functioning does not change the score.

0–4 Minimal5–9 Mild10–14 Moderate15–19 Moderately severe20–27 Severe

These questionnaires support reflection and conversations with a health professional. They do not establish or rule out a diagnosis. If symptoms persist or affect daily life, seek advice whatever your score.

Privacy and saved results

Answers and scoring stay in your browser. This site has no accounts, analytics, advertising, cookies, third-party scripts or external fonts. It does not send your answers or scores to us. External source and support links open other websites only when you choose them.

Saving is optional. Once enabled, it applies to all three questionnaires and stores only the questionnaire, version, date and total score in localStorage. Corrections update the current result. Choose Start again for a new result. You can turn saving off without deleting history.

History does not sync across browsers or devices. Someone using your browser profile can see it. Clearing site data or ending a private browsing session can erase it.

GitHub Pages, the intended host, logs visitor IP addresses for security. Answers and results are never included in page URLs or requests.
